# Code of Conduct

CAVE is a technical community where people should be able to contribute without harassment, intimidation, or exclusion.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while still addressing concrete impact.
- Make room for different backgrounds, experience levels, and communication styles.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Accept maintainer decisions and move disagreement into the documented governance process.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, doxxing, retaliation, and sustained personal attacks are not tolerated in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer using a private contact method on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not include sensitive conduct reports in public issues. Maintainers will acknowledge a report, review available evidence, minimize disclosure, and choose a proportionate response. Responses may include a warning, content removal, temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion.

Anyone directly involved in a report must recuse themselves from deciding it when another maintainer is available. Good-faith reports and participation in an investigation must not be retaliated against.
